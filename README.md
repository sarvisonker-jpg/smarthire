# Career Compass

Build a smart and user-friendly platform that helps students and freshers discover the right internships and jobs based on their skills, education, career interests, location, experience, and work preferences. The vision is to make job searching more personalized, simple, and career-focused by providing opportunity match scores, AI-powered recommendations, skill-gap analysis, and guidance to improve their chances of getting selected.
