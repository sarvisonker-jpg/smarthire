# SmartHire – AI-Assisted Career Recommendation Platform

SmartHire is a student-focused web platform designed to simplify internship and job exploration. It helps students explore relevant opportunities, understand potential skill gaps, and plan their career development.

🔗 **Live Website:** [https://career-finder-ai-52.lovable.app](https://career-finder-ai-52.lovable.app)

---

##  Project Objectives

- Simplify internship and job opportunity exploration.
- Help students explore opportunities based on their skills, interests, and preferences.
- Provide match scores and explanations for opportunities.
- Identify potential skill gaps.
- Offer a career development roadmap.
- Support career exploration through an AI Career Assistant interface.

---

##  Key Features

- **Student Profile:** Enter relevant educational and career preferences.
- **Opportunity Recommendations:** Explore internship and job listings.
- **Match Score Breakdown:** View factors associated with opportunity matching.
- **Skill Gap Analyzer:** Identify skills that may need improvement.
- **Career Roadmap:** Explore steps to improve career readiness.
- **AI Career Assistant:** Interface for career-related guidance.
- **Smart Filters:** Filter opportunities using available criteria such as role, location, skills, and work mode.
